**3. Techniques to Enhance Behavior (Beyond Basic Training):**

* **Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF):**  This is where humans rate the model’s outputs. This feedback is used to refine the 
model's responses, making them more aligned with human preferences.
* **Prompt Engineering:**  Crafting very specific and clear prompts can have a huge impact on the output. Experiment with different phrasing, 
and add constraints.
* **Temperature Tuning:** Adjusting the “temperature” setting in the model can control the randomness of the output – higher temperature = 
more creative, but potentially less consistent.

**4. Monitoring and Evaluation:**

* **Track Performance:** Keep an eye on the model's performance – accuracy, coherence, relevance, and the overall quality of its responses.
* **Human Review:** Always have a human review the model's output, especially during the initial stages.
